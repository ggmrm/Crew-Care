Crew Care

CrewCare is a simple wellness tracking app that’s purpose is to monitor the well being and health of astronauts during space missions.
The app allows astronauts to record their daily mood, stress level, sleep, energy, nutrition, and biological measurements. The app basically helps them track and log their wellness information in an organized way throughout a mission.

How to use the application:
The astronaut should firstly open the CrewCare application, and then enter their daily wellness information such as their mood, sleep, nutrition, health measurements like blood pressure and more, then submit the information to then view the summary of the astronaut’s wellness and health which will be saved.
